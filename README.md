Costa Verde — a 100% original 2D top-down open-world crime game for make.fun's gta6-01 bounty. Marco Solis owes Mama Rosa, and the only way out is through her five jobs: steal a car, pick up a package at the Portside docks, run a hot delivery across town, lose the heat, and pull the big score at Costa Verde Trust. Explore a sun-soaked coastal city on foot and by car, with traffic, pedestrians, a wanted level and police who chase you down, combat, a HUD with a live minimap, and a save that keeps your progress. No build step, no network — just static files and a browser.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — WASD/arrows to move and drive, E to enter or exit a car, SPACE to punch, mouse click to shoot, SHIFT to run, P to pause, M for sound, R to restart.
